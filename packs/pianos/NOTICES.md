# Notices: pianos

## Salamander Grand Piano V3

Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm (https://rytmenpinne.wordpress.com/sounds-and-such/salamander-grandpiano/),
a Yamaha C5 recorded at 48 kHz/24-bit. SFZ+FLAC edition by Roberto for FreePats
(https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html).

Released under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence (CC BY 3.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/); on 4 March 2022 the author dedicated it to the
public domain ("As of 4.3.2022, this is now public domain! Have fun with it, it's yours and
noones!"). We keep crediting him as CC BY 3.0 requires: the samples are by Alexander Holm, and
daw retunes and level-matches them in its zone map without changing the audio.

## Upright Piano KW

Upright Piano KW by Gonzalo and Roberto for FreePats

44.1 kHz/24-bit. Published under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 public domain dedication
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); the archive includes the legal code (`cc0.txt`).
