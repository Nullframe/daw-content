# Orchestral (CC0): notices

`orchestral-cc0-v1.tar` holds recorded orchestral samples from two CC0 libraries, trimmed,
looped (sustains) and losslessly re-encoded as FLAC by
[`packs/orchestral-cc0/build.py`](https://github.com/Nullframe/daw-content/tree/main/packs/orchestral-cc0).
The pack is **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication). Each upstream licence text is in the archive's
`licenses/` folder; per-file sources (repository, commit, path, sha256) and processing are in
`provenance.json`.

## VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition (VSCO 2 CE)

- By Versilian Studios LLC (Sam Gossner) and Ivy Audio (Simon Dalzell); sample cutting by Elan
  Hickler (Soundemote).
- https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE at commit `440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e`
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (`LICENSE` at that commit).
- Courtesy credit: the library's `Readme.txt` asks users to "provide credit to Versilian
  Studios/Sam Gossner, and/or Ivy Audio/Simon Dalzell where applicable, and link to the VSCO: CE
  homepage" (http://vis.versilstudios.net/vsco-community.html). This is a request, not a licence
  condition; we are glad to give it. It also asks that the samples not be sold directly: this
  pack is free.
- Used: violin, viola and cello sections and the solo double bass (sustain with vibrato,
  spiccato, pizzicato, tremolo); French horn, trumpet, tenor trombone, tuba (sustain,
  staccato); flute, oboe, clarinet, bassoon (sustain, staccato).

- By Versilian Studios LLC (Sam Gossner) and contributors.

- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (`LICENSE` at that commit; the README: "no royalties, no credit,

- Used: concert harp; glockenspiel, xylophone (hard mallets), marimba, vibraphone (soft and hard
